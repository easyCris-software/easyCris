# easyCris Commercial Offerings

Status: draft pending owner/counsel approval

easyCris Core is planned for release under Apache-2.0. The commercial offerings
described here are separate from the Apache-2.0 rights granted for Core.

## Apache Core

The planned Apache-covered Core includes the formula engine, statistical and
RNA-seq analysis functionality, public-safe application source, project format
needed by Core, and ordinary static plot copy/export behavior.

Users do not need a commercial license to use, copy, modify, or redistribute
Apache-covered Core under the Apache-2.0 terms once Core is published.

## Commercial Offerings

easyCris may offer paid or separately licensed offerings, including:

- official signed Windows installers;
- easyCris Pro Office Integration for editable Office/OLE embedding;
- support;
- sponsorships;
- managed services;
- warranties or indemnities;
- enterprise agreements;
- priority issue response or implementation services.

## easyCris Pro Office Integration

easyCris Pro Office Integration is planned as a separately licensed proprietary
component for editable Office/OLE behavior, including COM activation,
structured-storage clipboard objects, registration, repair, diagnostics, and
Pro-only packaging/tests.

Pro terms must not restrict rights granted for Apache-covered Core.

## Official Builds And Branding

Official signed builds may combine Apache-covered Core with separately licensed
Pro components. Official easyCris names, logos, signing certificates, release
channels, and updater metadata are not granted by the Apache-2.0 source license
unless a separate trademark or branding policy says otherwise.

This document is a draft for planning. It must be reviewed with the open-source
