# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and constructive collaboration environment.

## Expected Behavior

1. Be respectful and professional.
2. Focus on technical issues, not personal attacks.
3. Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
4. Accept feedback and resolve disagreements constructively.

## Unacceptable Behavior

1. Harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.
2. Personal attacks or intimidation.
3. Deliberate disruption of project collaboration.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation for violations.

Report conduct concerns to `hello@easycris.com`.
